# Round 2 — gpt — Engineering feasibility

**Read:** DEBATE_BRIEF, my r1-gpt, every other available R1 (beam, gemini, grok, kimi, sonnet), and VERDICT. r1-deepseek was absent. Round 2 only; no application code or changes to the provisional verdict. These are comparative votes on existing directions, for the human team to choose and shape—not an AI-originated solution decision.

## Agreement, disagreement, concessions

- **Flame: retain first place, with beam + kimi's constraints.** Agree with kimi that extraction plus qualitative evidence relevance gives both models meaningful work; agree with beam that an experiment comparison and source figure are a stronger first screen than a naked ranked bibliography. Concede to grok/sonnet that existing rankers invalidate any automatic novelty claim. They do not make a curated evidence comparison unshippable. The critical path is three trustworthy source records, not an archive browser. Ranking investigation applicability must never become a prediction of lunar fire risk. Numeric oxygen/pressure matching stays in code; combustion-regime transfer remains an explicitly uncertain interpretation.
- **Trend: promote to second, against sonnet's 5/10.** Agree with grok and gemini: a reproducible small numerical result is a substantial engineering advantage. Optional AI is an acceptable trade-off; adding a model does not improve the p-value. Concede sonnet's creativity/presentation objection: a generic Giovanni clone is weak. Existing baseline/seasonality/non-result directions can demonstrate a specific interpretive improvement without building a new analysis platform. Reject VERDICT's shorthand “Giovanni CSV plus seasonal Mann–Kendall” as sufficient methodology: seasonal handling does not automatically fix serial dependence [R2].
- **Jukebox: retain third, concede the AI-depth critique.** Kimi and grok are right that a synthesizer owns the central transformation; Jev as conductor is expendable. Beam/gemini correctly make an intelligible mapping and equivalent controls the acceptance criteria. My R1 8/10 assumed two cached clips and one audio engine, not six tracks, live Lyria, TTS and per-frame inference. A useful deterministic product with modest reviewed AI assistance beats a fragile four-model demonstration.
- **Storytelling: retain fourth, narrowly over Analogs.** Agree with beam on the small static delivery surface and human-scale explanation; concede to kimi/sonnet/grok that it is the weakest named-stack fit. Disagree with grok's blanket 3/10: human-authored, sourced content does not inherit the failure of an AI-written rover voice. The actual cost is writing/review and rights clearance. No child-facing Gemini runtime; a separately operated adult authoring workflow is distinct from merely hiding calls behind a teacher screen (gemini's correction). If two live models are a hard requirement rather than stack goals, this option should fall out.
- **Analogs: move to reserve despite sonnet/kimi's enthusiasm.** Purpose-specific qualitative judgments are a plausible AI role; I concede that to kimi. But an expert-approved rubric, commensurable evidence and an unfamiliar candidate are unresolved prerequisites. My R1 known-site comparator can become too easy by sidestepping the challenge's new-analog objective, as gemini notes. Low GitHub counts do not solve that. Promote only after a domain reviewer accepts one candidate's match/mismatch evidence; embeddings alone cannot pass.
- **Hot Spots: dissent from its 4/5 provisional support.** Agree on user value and the importance of sensor seams; reject treating a ratio, FRP sum or sensor-specific z-score as established harmonization. Held-out overlap agreement and observation/missingness treatment are the product's hard part. A fire CSV plus calendar is not a completed challenge response. No burn-driver labels without evidence. The SNPP notice does not settle Aqua status or validate historical comparisons.
- **Lunar/SPHEREx: disagree with sonnet's fallback recommendation when the team lacks expertise.** No-key Horizons and anonymous IRSA access reduce authentication risk, not terrain-mask or epoch/wavelength/PSF risk. A beautiful Sun-up/Earth-down example cannot substitute for a checked terrain horizon; five named sky targets are acceptance cases, not five already working data pairs. Both remain reserves, behind proof of their scientific pipeline. Farms likewise requires agronomic review; SAR requires comparable real NISAR acquisitions. None gains feasibility from model confidence.

**Correction to VERDICT:** “model confidence” is still too loose a label. Jev Choice/Score confidence measures distribution concentration; it is neither top-answer correctness nor scientific validity, and Noul has no separate confidence field [R3]. Display the criterion and model distribution only where they help; don't market a weekend smoke test as calibration. Kimi's fallback-adapter warning also stands: Decision 1 is not a model-string replacement or independent validation.

## Revised shortlist and final ranked vote

Ranking weights a defensible shipped result in ~40 hours for 3–5 people, including integration/review, then genuine stack utility. It is not a prediction of awards. The first-two-hour gates below are **alternative finalist spikes**, not four simultaneous projects.

| Vote | Challenge / bounded existing direction | Single biggest risk | First 2 hours: evidence required to proceed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Flame in Freefall** — 8–12 curated investigations; scenario-conditioned relevance with cited comparison | Extraction/rubric errors produce an authoritative but unsupported ranking | Retrieve three representative PSI-linked records/papers as actual bytes; manually verify fuel, oxygen basis, pressure, flow, gravity and one exact source span per record. Include one poor-match or missing-evidence case. Run one real extraction + typed judgment, compare with a transparent filter baseline, and inspect any changed ordering. **Pass:** three traceable records, no unsupported displayed measurements, and a defensible qualitative ranking question. **Fail:** only titles/metadata accessible or no reviewer can check the evidence; do not substitute a chatbot. |
| **2** | **Earth System Trend Detective** — one variable, two cached regions, explicit non-result | Spurious significance from dependence, seasonality or selective windows | Export and parse the actual NASA series; check units, timestamps, missingness and sampling. Reproduce slope/significance on an independent known fixture, then inspect seasonality/autocorrelation and run a justified sensitivity analysis on the real series. **Pass:** reproducible source-to-result path with method/assumptions and export; disagreement is shown rather than hidden. **Fail:** no usable series or no defensible inference; don't replace the test with model prose. |
| **3** | **Earth Information Jukebox** — two cached EIC clips, one explicit mapping | Sound tracks editing/color artifacts rather than the stated data feature | Obtain one real frame sequence and its credits; isolate the mapped region and verify whether values or only image features are available. Produce a short synchronized audio sample; test play/pause/seek and a keyboard-only path. Ask a listener what changed using the stated mapping. **Pass:** reproducible, understandable audible change and an honest image-feature label if physical values are unavailable. **Fail:** soundtrack without an inspectable mapping; remove live music dependencies rather than masking the gap. |
| **4** | **Discarded Equipment Storytelling** — one mission/site or six human-authored artifacts | Content/asset provenance cannot be reviewed quickly enough | A human writes two short artifact stories; another checks each factual sentence against mission records and clears the selected images' rights/credits. Check the intended age/architecture boundary before selecting any runtime provider. **Pass:** two complete, sourced stories with transcripts/alt text and static delivery, with a credible path to the remaining content. **Fail:** invented quotations, unclear rights or dependence on live child-facing Gemini. |

## Shared engineering gate and evidence

In the first hour, test the team's actual credentials, exact model IDs and intended payloads; credentials never enter logs. If models are required, prove the extraction→decision contract and one forced failure/replay path before UI polish. Code owns numeric constraints, transformations, statistics and audio scheduling. Reviewed cached responses are labeled; source evidence and a deterministic baseline remain usable on timeout. Raw scientific imagery feeds analysis/models; dither is presentation-only. Reserve the final 6–8 elapsed hours for integration, accessible controls, credits/licenses, SI disclosures and the ≤30-second/≤7-slide submission. Human ownership of the concept remains required.

## 3. Biggest risk and the first two hours

### 1. Hot spots — fake harmonization, or no calendar at all

**Risk.** GPT's objection and the challenge text pull in opposite directions. A coefficient with no overlap test fails Validity. A page of caveats and no matrix fails Relevance. Either one is enough to lose.

**Two hours.** With a real `MAP_KEY`, read `data_availability` for `MODIS_SP`, `VIIRS_SNPP_SP`, `VIIRS_NOAA20_SP`, and `VIIRS_NOAA21_SP`. Pull one AOI across one overlap year, counts by sensor, not a 25-year fit. Write on the fixture: "detections, not fires, not burned area." If the months after 2 Nov 2026 do not show the SNPP drop, or the recent standard products 404, do not build the seam story. Do not estimate a harmonization coefficient in these two hours.

### 2. Trend detective — a p-value the team does not believe, or no CSV

**Risk.** The demo that wins is a non-significant or sign-flipping result. The demo the team will be tempted to record is a steep line. Autocorrelation and the seasonal cycle are how that steep line becomes a false 5 on Validity. The other failure is Earthdata login, which round 1 never passed.

**Two hours.** One monthly CSV on disk, from Giovanni or an already-cached product, with the official long name and units. Run one seasonal Mann–Kendall. Keep the result even if it is not significant. If no series can be exported or reused inside two hours, leave the challenge. Do not call a model to decorate an empty chart.

### 3. Flame — a lunar score on the wrong flame, or a number that is not in the paper

**Risk.** Not the repo count. An accepted oxygen or pressure that Hydrogen wrote into both the field and the "quote," or a habitat rank that treats 0 g and 1 g as a lunar cabin. An SME stops at that slide.

**Two hours.** Open three investigation JSON records and one NTRS PDF. A second person finds fuel, oxygen, pressure, and gravity environment in the source, or the field stays blank. Confirm the download plan does not include FLEX or ACME. If three quotable records do not exist in two hours, stop (gpt's gate). Put "partial gravity: not in PSI" on the fixture before any rank.

### 4. Lunar — a smooth horizon, or the wrong mission

**Risk.** The hero (Sun up, Earth down) is only a hero if a second person can recompute it. A spherical horizon sold as terrain-aware south-pole planning fails the challenge's own subject. "Artemis III landing site" is false. "Artemis IV in 2028" is not a sentence on the NASA page this pass read.

**Two hours.** One site, one date, the Horizons request written down, Sun and Earth elevation matched to about a tenth of a degree by someone who did not write the query. The fixture says "spherical horizon" or it shows a LOLA mask that was checked. If the elevations do not match, do not record the sentence. If no mask exists, keep the clock and delete every "terrain-aware" claim. Do not start analogs in the leftover time.

### Contingent: jukebox — sonifying the colormap

**Risk.** A pleasant mapping of painted pixels. That fails the challenge even if the audio is accessible and the models are unused.

**Two hours, only if the top four stall.** One SVS frame set. Decide whether the pixel is a documented geophysical value. If it is only color, either label the sound "image luminance, not the measurement" or kill the direction. No Lyria call. No TTS in the demo path.
